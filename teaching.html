What I teach

Topics I teach as lectures, workshops or course modules for undergraduate, postgraduate and industry audiences — each built on published research and industrial case studies.

Digital twins & Industry 4.0

What a digital twin is — and what it is not; white-, grey- and black-box twins; how to build, validate and deploy one, with industrial cases from pumps and generators to gas turbines and battery storage.

Taught at SRM University-AP and BML Munjal University

Five lenses on digital twins

System identification & nonlinear modelling

Linear versus nonlinear identification, excitation design with multisines, nonparametric detection of nonlinear distortions and polynomial nonlinear state-space (PNLSS) models — with Li-ion batteries as the running example.

From my PhD at Vrije Universiteit Brussel

PhD stage of the research journey

Time-series analysis & stochastic grey-box modelling

Stochastic differential equations, Kalman filtering and maximum-likelihood estimation; building grey-box models step by step, with cases from building energy flexibility and resin infusion in wind-blade moulding.

Guest lectures in Advanced Time Series Analysis, DTU Compute

Postdoc stage at DTU

Battery analytics & BESS digital twins

State of health and power fade, coupled electro-thermal models, anomaly detection without labelled failures, and running analytics at the edge next to deterministic control.

From industrial research at Siemens Energy

Battery storage case studies

Surrogate models, prognostics & AI/ML for engineering

Gaussian-process and reduced-order surrogate models, FMECA and predictive maintenance, and remaining-useful-life estimation for fleets — from generator and gas-turbine case studies.

From industrial research at Siemens Energy

Gas turbine & generator case studies
