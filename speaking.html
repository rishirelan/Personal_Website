“The next decade will be decided between the boxes.”

Digital Twins and AI for the Energy Transition: From Prototype to Production

Most digital twins never leave the pilot stage — they end up as snapshots, black boxes, orphans or toys. Drawing on published work on gas turbines, generators and battery storage, this talk shows what separates a living twin from a simulation, how much model is enough, and why data orchestration now decides success.

Deploying AI in Battery Energy Storage: What Breaks Between the Lab and the Field

On the grid, a battery is a living cyber-physical asset. Built around five questions a battery can’t answer alone — its health, its heat, its anomalies, whether the answer can arrive in time, and what to do — this talk shows what published AI and digital-twin methods already achieve, and where deployment breaks.

Dr.ir. Rishi Relan is an engineering and R&D leader who bridges artificial intelligence and the physical machines that power the world. His expertise spans Digital Twins, Cyber-Physical Systems and Industrial AI/ML. Over a 20-year career across six countries, he has led global initiatives across Siemens Energy’s Gas Services and Grid Technologies businesses. He founded the Battery Advanced AI Technology Lab at Siemens Energy India and served as Global Technical Advisor for Condition-Based Service Intervention, the company’s predictive-maintenance programme.

He holds a PhD in Engineering Sciences from Vrije Universiteit Brussel (University of Brussels), Belgium. His further qualifications include an Advanced Master’s in Artificial Intelligence from KU Leuven, Belgium; an MPhil in Control Systems from the University of Sheffield, UK; an MSc in Mechatronics from Ravensburg-Weingarten University of Applied Sciences, Germany; and a BTech in Control Engineering from Kurukshetra University, India. His research has taken him to ETH Zürich, Switzerland; DTU, Denmark; and the Rolls-Royce University Technology Centre, UK.

He has authored more than 70 publications and holds multiple trade secrets and invention disclosures in battery AI. His honours include the EPSRC–Rolls-Royce Dorothy Hodgkin Postgraduate Award, the Applied Energy Highly Cited Paper Award, the Siemens Ingenuity Award and the IEEE SEFET 2025 Best Paper Award.

A sought-after speaker on industrial AI, he was an Invited Spotlight Speaker at ASME IMECE 2026 and delivered a keynote address at ICAMDM 2025. At the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi, he gave an invited talk at the ISME Golden Jubilee Industry–Academia Conclave in October 2026. Also at IIT Delhi, he served as an invited panellist at the ChemRD² symposium that same month and, in 2018, delivered the IEEE Technical Seminar in the Department of Electrical Engineering. In November 2026, he delivered an invited lecture on AI/ML and Digital Twins in the Research & Innovation Dialogue Series of the Indo-German Science & Technology Centre (IGSTC). He is an Adjunct Professor and Industrial Advisory Board member at BML Munjal University, a former reviewer for the European Research Council, APAC Lead for ASME IGTI Associated Events, and a founding member of ASME India’s Cyber-Physical Systems and AI/ML group.
